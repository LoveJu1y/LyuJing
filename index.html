My name is LyuJing. I received my bachelor's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University. I am currently a second-year joint Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences and the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Zhongyuan Wang and Prof. Xiaoguang Zhao.

My research interests focus on embodied intelligence, vision-language-action models, world models, and agentic robotics.

Currently, I am at the Embodied Intelligence Research Center of the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence, advised by Pengwei Wang.

I am always happy to discuss research and collaboration. You can find me on GitHub.

Selected Work

2026
Orca: The World is in Your Mind.

Core Contributor Technical report 2026

Project|Paper|Code

Orca is a world model that shifts the learning target from next-token, next-frame, or next-action prediction to next-state prediction. It learns a unified world latent representation from multimodal world signals and supports downstream readouts for language, vision, and action.
